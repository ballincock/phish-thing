1. Our Mission

TurtleStew is a free fishing platform for all, intended to provide a comprehensive set of information and services to any and all fishermen. We love fishing and believe that it is a healthy and fun past-time that not only allows people to be happy with their accomplishments, but to connect with others and enjoy life to the fullest. It is important to us that this remains free.

2. Server Infrastructure

This platform is individual made and owned, done completely in the time between shifts at work and fishing sessions. While we intend for this to be free forever, the owner works a minimum wage job while simultaneously handling production and server costs, which add up a lot faster than they would like. In order to keep it this way, an optional donation would help us maintain this goal. Entirely up to the discretion of the user, any and all support would be greatly appreciated. Thank you kindly!
